Why instruct a property agent to sell your property?

Selling a property is complex and time-consuming. A good agent brings market knowledge, experience, and the ability to present your home effectively.

  • Recommend an accurate asking price based on local market conditions.
  • Use effective marketing tactics to reach the biggest audience.
  • Present your property professionally online.
  • Provide performance reports and improvement advice.
  • Reduce stress by handling negotiations, exchange, and completion.

Benefits of using a letting agent

A letting agent handles marketing, ensures details are correct, and helps especially if you’re new to being a landlord.

  • Reduced stress and time saved.
  • Guidance and expert advice.
  • Support if the property is far from where you live.
  • Professional market valuation and realistic rental expectations.

How to choose an estate agent

Choose an agent you trust and who has your best interests at heart. Ask them:

  • How and where your property will be advertised.
  • How your property compares to others on the market.
  • For examples of previous sales similar to yours.
  • About their fees, contract terms, and exclusivity period.
